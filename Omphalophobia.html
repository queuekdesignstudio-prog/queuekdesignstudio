Ompalophobia

School Project / Clothing Brand

You are terrified of being separated from the mother and, weird as it sounds, that might be causing your Omphalophobia, your intense aversion to touching or witnessing a navel. Because every time you are looking at one, it acts as a reminder of your very first scar, the moment they literally separate your body of that of your mother’s. Hence, my design represents a capital “O” in the form of the dual cellular entities on the verge of mitosis.
