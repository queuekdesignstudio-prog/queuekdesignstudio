One Minute To Catch Up And Forget

School Project / Packaging

In 2002, two scientists measured the spectra of about two hundred thousand galaxies and concluded that the average color of the universe was... this. So if we squeezed the entire universe into a small box, its glow would be this color. And as if that weren’t enough, they called it a «cosmic latte».

One Minute to Catch Up and Forget is a short music piece by Joakim Bouaziz that compresses some of the biggest hits of the past 50 years into a single, condensed experience.
