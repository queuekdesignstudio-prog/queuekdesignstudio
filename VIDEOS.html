Agfa is now kitsch better.

School Project / Rebrand

Rebranding of AGFA’s logo, exploring a kitsch-inspired visual direction. The project embraces exaggeration, nostalgia, and bold color to reinterpret the brand through a more playful and expressive lens.

Drawing from vintage aesthetics and retro visual culture, the new identity shifts away from minimal precision and moves toward a more emotional and character-driven approach. The result is a logo that feels both familiar and unexpected — celebrating imperfection, excess, and visual intensity.

Unleash

School Project / Event Promo Video

Promotional clip created for the techno event “Unleash Strike 22". The concept focuses on intensity, rhythm, and release — visually translating the raw energy of techno music into a fast-paced, immersive experience.

Through dark tones, sharp contrasts, and rapid motion, the video builds a sense of pressure and anticipation, reflecting the atmosphere of a live set. The visuals aim to feel aggressive and hypnotic, capturing the moment where control is lost and energy takes over.

Designed for social media, the clip is structured to immediately grab attention and communicate the identity of the event within seconds.

Ever Alone Again?

School Project / Retro-Futuristic Motion Study

A 3D motion exploration built around retro-inspired aesthetics. The video experiments with form, texture, and movement, drawing from early digital visuals and nostalgic visual languages.

Through the use of bold colors, synthetic materials, and stylized motion, the piece creates a sense of familiarity while remaining abstract and open-ended. It doesn’t follow a specific narrative, but instead focuses on atmosphere and visual rhythm.

The result is a short study on how retro influences can be reinterpreted through contemporary 3D tools, blending past and present into a unified visual experience.

Athens isn't Athens.

School Project / Promotional Video

A promotional piece for Athens, Georgia, playing with the familiar name to challenge expectations. At first glance, the title suggests the historic city of Athens, Greece — but the project reveals a different place with its own identity.

Using Athens, Georgia’s most iconic landmarks and lifestyle experiences—from nightlife to cultural spots—the city is presented as more than a destination. It becomes an experience in motion.
